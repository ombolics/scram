How to play
  • Keep thermal power, fuel temperature and steam pressure inside limits while the grid asks for power.
  • Cut demand and watch the plant fight back: steam pressure rises, coolant heats, power falls on its own.
  • Hit SCRAM any time. Shutdown is not cooling: decay heat keeps going for hours.
  • Compare Gen I, II and III+ with the same fault, for example Blackout at 300x speed.
  • Start-up: pick Hot standby, set speed to 30x, pull rods out slowly, then raise demand.
